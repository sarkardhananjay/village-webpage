Put your village photos in this folder (jpg or png).

Suggested names (match the commented <img> lines in index.html):
  cover.jpg          - big hero cover photo
  school.jpg         - the primary school
  market-haat.jpg    - weekly haat
  market-bazaar.jpg  - main bazaar
  temple.jpg         - the temple
  river.jpg          - river / landscape
  nature-1.jpg ... nature-6.jpg  - nature gallery
  life-1.jpg ...     - village life cards

To show a photo, open index.html, find the matching photo box, and
un-comment the <img ...> line inside it (remove the <!-- and -->).

Tip: resize big photos to about 1600px wide before adding, so the site loads fast.
